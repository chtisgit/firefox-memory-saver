
v0.2.0:
* Added a Memory Saver submenu to the tab context menu.
* Protect an individual tab with "Do not discard this tab". Per-tab protection
  follows the tab across normal Firefox restarts and session restoration.
* Protect a website with "Do not discard <domain>". This adds the domain to the
  existing excluded-domain settings, and both context-menu actions can be
  reversed with their corresponding "Allow discarding" action.
* Added Sleeping tabs and Protected tabs views to the popup, with live counts
  for both. Sleeping tabs remains selected by default.
* The Protected tabs view lists every open tab protected by a manual rule,
  protected domain, pinned-tab setting, audible-tab setting, or special-page
  safety rule, together with its last-accessed time and applicable protection
  reasons.
* Increased the popup width to give tab titles and protection details more room.

v0.1.2:
* Manifest changes.

v0.1.1:
* Automatically unload inactive tabs after a configurable amount of time.
* Choose how frequently Memory Saver checks for unused tabs.
* Keep a minimum number of tabs loaded in each window.
* Protect pinned tabs and tabs playing audio.
* Protect specific websites and domains from being unloaded.
* Manually unload eligible tabs across Firefox or only other tabs in the current window.
* View all currently sleeping tabs in one convenient list.
* Restore or close sleeping tabs directly from the popup.
* Optionally record local foreground active-tab time with configurable retention.
* Clear locally stored activity data at any time.
* No advertisements, tracking, analytics, or data transmission.
* No access to webpage contents.
* Open-source software.
